How Smart Money Confirmation Works

LONG REVERSAL: Support test + bullish candle + buying pressure + volume confirmation + BTC bullish alignment.

LONG BREAKOUT: Resistance break + strong taker buying + volume expansion + BTC not bearish.

SHORT REJECTION: Resistance test + bearish candle + selling pressure + volume confirmation + BTC bearish alignment.

SHORT BREAKDOWN: Support break + strong taker selling + volume expansion + BTC not bullish.

Taker buying/selling is a market-flow proxy. It cannot independently prove whale accumulation or distribution. Open interest, liquidation and order-book analysis would require additional data.
