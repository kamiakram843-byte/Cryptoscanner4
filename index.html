How to read the signals

LONG reversal: A possible support bounce supported by multi-timeframe confirmation. Wait for the 5m reclaim before entry.

LONG breakout: Wait for a candle close above resistance with volume and lower-timeframe confirmation.

SHORT rejection: A possible resistance rejection; wait for a 5m bearish rejection.

SHORT breakdown: Wait for a candle close below support with volume; a retest rejection can offer a less rushed entry.

Trend model: 4H and 2H establish direction; 1H and 15m confirm; 5m is only an entry trigger. Trend weights: 15m 15%, 1h 25%, 2h 25%, 4h 35%.

BTC alignment: Strong altcoin signals against BTC remain visible but are marked COUNTER BTC and receive a score penalty.

BTC crash: Only SHORT signals are displayed. BTC upside breakout: Only LONG signals are displayed.

Support ETA: A volatility-based estimate from current ATR and support distance. It is not an exact arrival time.

Confirmed reversal: Requires support to hold, a bullish closed 5m candle reclaiming its EMA, RSI above 50, improving 15m bias and buying pressure. A confirmation can fail.

Bell alerts: Tap Enable loud alerts once. The browser may require notification permission. Acknowledge stops the repeating bell. The tab must remain open.

This scanner does not identify individual whales or access private exchange orders. Forecast ranges are not validated probabilities. Model score is a confluence score, not win probability. Always backtest and paper trade before risking funds.
