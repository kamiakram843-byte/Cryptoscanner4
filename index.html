Signal Rules

BUY: 4H context must not be strongly bearish; 1H must show a pullback near support/lower Bollinger Band and the 50 EMA slope must not be clearly down. If price swept below the 1H lower band, it must close back above that band. 15M must confirm a structural shift above a recent swing high. The scanner rejects buys when price is over 5% above the 1H 20 EMA, near its resistance proxy, or 15M RSI exceeds 75.

SELL: uses the mirrored logic: rejection near 1H upper band/resistance, no clearly rising 1H 50 EMA, a close back below the upper band after a sweep, and a 15M structural break below recent swing lows. Long and short signals are directional alerts only.

BTC filter: altcoin BUY signals are blocked during a detected sharp BTC drop or aggressive vertical pump. A controlled BTC trend or sideways condition can pass the BTC gate. This is a rule-based proxy, not a complete market-regime model.

All signal triggers use closed candles. The scan uses public Binance Spot market data and does not execute trades. R:R, stop loss and targets depend on the current candle structure and can be invalidated by gaps, slippage or fast volatility.

Public Binance market data · TradingView charts · RSS news · No orders are placed. API limits, internet access and Binance regional availability may affect scanning.
