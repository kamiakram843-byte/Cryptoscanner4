Strategy & Risk Settings

BUY candidates require a bullish 15m candle, a pullback/rebound setup, and a score threshold. SELL candidates require bearish confirmation. BTC conditions influence scores but do not guarantee altcoin direction. Only closed candles are used for entry confirmation; targets use the latest available bands.

Risk notice: This is a market scanner, not financial advice. Signals may fail, bands move as new candles form, and high-volume pairs can still be volatile.
