Scanner Methodology and Recommendations

LONG: Price near 15M Lower Bollinger Band, RSI 6 increasing, bullish candle and reversal confirmation.
SHORT: Price near 15M Upper Bollinger Band, RSI 6 decreasing, bearish candle and reversal confirmation.
BREAKOUT: Closed candle above previous resistance, increased volume and increasing RSI.
BREAKDOWN: Closed candle below previous support, increased volume and decreasing RSI.

Indicator settings: Bollinger Bands 20,2. RSI 6 for entry, RSI 14 for confirmation. ATR 14. Volume average 20 candles.

Recommendations: Prefer trades with 2:1 or better risk/reward. Prefer 1H and 4H confirmation. Avoid chasing extended candles. Wait for candle close and, where possible, a successful retest. Use position sizing based on your maximum acceptable loss.
