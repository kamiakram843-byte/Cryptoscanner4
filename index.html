Trading Style Guide

⚡ SCALPING

5-minute entry trigger with 15-minute trend confirmation. Requires active monitoring. Suggested active window: 6 PM–10 PM PKT.

📊 DAY TRADING

15-minute entry trigger, with 1-hour and 4-hour trend confirmation. Intended for same-day trades. Suggested monitoring window: 1 PM–10 PM PKT.

🌊 SWING TRADING

4-hour entry trigger with daily trend confirmation. Trades may last several days or weeks. Review after daily candle close.

🛡️ RISK MANAGEMENT

TP1 = 1R, TP2 = 2R, TP3 = 3R. Consider risking only a small portion of account equity per trade. These targets do not guarantee profit.
