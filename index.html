How signals are confirmed

BUY / LONG: BTC bullish confirmation first, then the altcoin must show a support bounce or confirmed bullish breakout. The scanner checks candle rejection, close confirmation, volume, RSI and MACD.

SELL / SHORT: BTC bearish confirmation first, then the altcoin must show a resistance rejection or confirmed bearish breakdown. The scanner checks upper wick, bearish close, volume, RSI and MACD.

WAIT: BTC is neutral/mixed, the coin has no confirmed setup, or the coin's direction disagrees with BTC.

Support and resistance are estimated from recent swing highs and lows. Signals are technical estimates, not guaranteed predictions or financial advice. Always verify levels and manage risk before trading.
