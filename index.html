How to Read the Scanner

Pullback: price is retracing from a recent extreme.
Rebound: price is moving away from the recent swing low or high.
PB/RB ratio: compares those two measured moves. It is not a win rate.
LONG / BUY: bullish trend, entry-zone proximity and closed-candle confirmation.
SHORT / EXIT: bearish trend, rejection and closed-candle confirmation.
BTC regime: a context filter, not a guarantee that altcoins follow BTC.

The scanner does not place trades. Verify levels, fees, slippage, liquidity, contract rules and funding before trading. Backtest the strategy before using real money.
