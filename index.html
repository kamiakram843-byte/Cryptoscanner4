How to read the signals

LONG reversal: A possible support bounce supported by 5m/15m/1h/4h alignment. Wait for the 5m reclaim before entry.

LONG breakout: Wait for a candle close above resistance with volume and lower-timeframe confirmation. Avoid chasing a stretched candle.

SHORT rejection: A possible resistance rejection; wait for a 5m bearish rejection/reclaim failure.

SHORT breakdown: Wait for a candle close below support with volume; a retest rejection can offer a less rushed entry.

1H/2H ranges are ATR-based scenarios, not exact targets or validated probabilities. Model score is a directional-strength score, not win probability. BTC is not used as a direction filter. OI, order-book depth, liquidations and verified whale-flow data are not included in this single-file version. Always backtest and paper trade before risking funds.
