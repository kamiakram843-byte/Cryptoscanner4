Strategy Rules

  • BTC 15m support is estimated from recent candle lows.
  • Bullish confirmation requires a green reversal candle, such as a bullish engulfing or a strong lower wick.
  • Bearish confirmation requires a resistance rejection and a red reversal candle.
  • Volume, 1h trend, RSI and reward-to-risk contribute to scoring.
  • Entry, stop-loss and targets are estimates based on candle structure and ATR.
  • BTC conditions filter all other coin signals; BTC support alone is not enough to trigger a trade.

Signals are educational estimates, not financial advice. This dashboard does not place orders. Verify liquidity, spread, funding, slippage and levels before trading.
