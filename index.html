Signal methodology

BUY/LONG candidates favor an uptrend, a pullback near EMA20/EMA50, and a recovering candle. SELL/SHORT candidates favor an extended price, resistance rejection, weakening momentum, and bearish confirmation. SL uses recent swing structure plus an ATR buffer. Targets use risk multiples of 1R, 2R and 3R. BTC weakness is shown as a warning context. This prototype does not guarantee that a dip will rebound or a peak will reject. All calculations use closed candles; signals can change on the next scan. Futures leverage, funding, slippage and liquidation risk are not modeled.
