HOW THE SIGNALS WORK
BTC confirmation: BTC 4H, 1H and 15M trends must all agree. If BTC is mixed or neutral, directional altcoin signals are blocked.
Spot: BUY means bullish confirmation; SELL means bearish conditions where an existing holding may be considered for an exit. Spot SELL is not a short position.
Futures: LONG requires bullish confirmation; SHORT requires bearish confirmation.
Coin confirmation: the coin's 4H, 1H and 15M trends must agree with BTC direction.
Support/resistance: calculated as the lowest low and highest high of the last 40 completed candles on each timeframe. These are reference zones, not guaranteed turning points.
Risk: Entry/SL/TP levels are estimates based on ATR. This scanner does not place orders.
