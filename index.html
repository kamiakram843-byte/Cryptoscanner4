How the scanner calculates levels
• Direction: EMA trend, recent swing structure, RSI, momentum and VWAP position.
• Confirmation: relative volume compared with recent candles; Futures also checks funding and open-interest change when available.
• Entry: a zone around the latest price and EMA20, adjusted using ATR.
• Stop loss: beyond recent swing structure with an ATR safety buffer.
• TP1 / TP2 / TP3: 1R, 2R and 3R from the entry midpoint, where R is the distance from entry to stop loss.
• A setup is shown only when its score and risk/reward checks pass. Otherwise it is WAIT.

Important: These are rule-based estimates, not guaranteed professional signals or financial advice. Public API data can be delayed or rate-limited. The scanner does not execute trades. Spot SELL is a bearish/exit warning, not a short-selling instruction.
