08 / STRICT STRATEGY & RISK RULES

BTC regime: BTC above its 200-day daily SMA = RISK-ON; below = RISK-OFF. This changes signal priority and blocks opposite-bias altcoin entries. If BTC data is unavailable, the scanner fails closed and does not approve new signals.

LONG trigger: 1H SMA20 above SMA50, current closed price above SMA20, and RSI crosses above 30 from at or below 30 on the latest two closed candles.

SHORT trigger: 1H SMA20 below SMA50, current closed price below SMA20, and RSI crosses below 70 from at or above 70 on the latest two closed candles.

Funding risk: Long entries are blocked when the symbol's latest available funding rate exceeds +0.05% per funding interval. Funding data missing or stale blocks a new signal. Open interest is contextual and does not independently establish whale accumulation or distribution.

Entry: latest market price at scan time. Stop loss: 2% from entry. Risk distance: R = absolute difference between entry and stop.

LONG: SL = EP × 0.98; TP1 = EP + R; TP2 = EP + 2R; TP3 = EP + 3.5R.
SHORT: SL = EP × 1.02; TP1 = EP − R; TP2 = EP − 2R; TP3 = EP − 3.5R.

Validation: LONG requires SL < EP < TP1 < TP2 < TP3. SHORT requires TP3 < TP2 < TP1 < EP < SL. Invalid prices are rejected.

Execution: signals are informational and are not executed automatically. Public API availability, fees, slippage, latency, and sudden market changes can affect results.
