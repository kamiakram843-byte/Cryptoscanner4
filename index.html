Signal definitions

A — Panic Dip (LONG)
Previous closed 15m candle closes below lower Bollinger Band with RSI below 30. Next closed candle returns inside the bands with a lower rejection wick.
B — Routine Low (LONG)
15m candle sweeps a confirmed 1h/4h pivot support and closes above it; lower wick at least 50% of body; RSI 30–42.
C — Panic Peak (SHORT)
Previous closed 15m candle closes above upper Bollinger Band with RSI above 75. Next closed candle returns inside and shows bearish RSI divergence.
D — Routine Peak (SHORT)
15m candle sweeps confirmed 1h/4h resistance and closes below it; upper wick at least 50% of body; volume below preceding green-candle average.

Pullback = reference level to watch for a retest. Rebound = close of the confirmed reversal candle. EP = breakout trigger just beyond the rejection candle. These are intentionally separate prices.
