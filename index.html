📖 Engine methodology and risk disclosure

BTC master engine: Uses BTC 15m, 1H and 4H klines, Bollinger Bands (20,2), taker pressure, order-book walls, funding, open interest and news sentiment to select exactly one scenario.

Scenario A: BTC pump-first plan. Altcoins can only generate SHORT cards if seller distribution or overhead resistance is present.

Scenario B: BTC drop-first plan. Altcoins can only generate LONG cards if buyer accumulation or support is present.

Conflict lock: Both buyer and seller conditions true means the coin is skipped. A Map keyed by symbol prevents duplicate active cards.

Beta: Covariance of coin 15m log returns divided by BTC 15m return variance over 32 completed candles.

Volume spike: Last completed 15m candle volume divided by the average volume of the preceding 20 completed candles.

Risk: Stop loss uses 1.2 × ATR. Targets are based on BTC's projected reversal multiplied by coin beta.
