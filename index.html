How to interpret the scanner

LONG reversal: Price is near a recent support area and shows bullish confirmation. Wait for a candle close and reclaim.

LONG breakout: Price has moved through recent resistance. Wait for a confirmed close and volume support; avoid chasing an extended candle.

SHORT rejection: Price is near resistance and shows bearish confirmation. Wait for a rejection candle.

SHORT breakdown: Price has moved below recent support. Wait for a confirmed close or retest rejection.

BTC normal: Altcoins are evaluated independently.

BTC crash / breakout: The scanner can temporarily hide opposing altcoin setups after its confirmation rules are met.

Model score: A technical setup score, not a win probability. It has not been calibrated against historical trades.

Risk: Entry, stop and targets are calculated estimates. Slippage, fees, funding, liquidity and sudden news can change actual results.
