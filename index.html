How the scanner qualifies a bearish setup

• 15-minute candles assess entry timing, momentum and pullbacks.

• 1-hour and 4-hour candles assess the broader trend.

• BTC's market regime warns of a sharp dip, possible rebound, sideways range or uptrend.

• Entry, stop and targets are calculated from recent candle structure and ATR volatility.

• “Confirmed” means technical conditions passed this program's rules. It does not mean a profitable trade is confirmed.

• The session label uses approximate UTC trading-session hours. Crypto trades 24/7; sessions are not exchange opening hours.
