How Futures confirmation works

• Price trend: EMA 20 / 50 / 200.
• Momentum: RSI 14 and MACD.
• Funding rate: identifies crowded long/short positioning pressure.
• Open interest: compares current open interest with its previous hourly reading.
• Long/short ratio: measures the proportion of long and short accounts.
• BUY / SELL are Spot signals. LONG / SHORT are Futures signals.
• Futures scores combine trend and derivatives confirmation. They are not win probabilities.
