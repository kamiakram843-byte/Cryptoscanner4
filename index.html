Signal Information

BUY requires lower Bollinger Band proximity or a recent lower-band rejection, bullish reversal evidence, and supporting momentum. SELL uses the opposite conditions near the upper band. A bullish EMA trend alone does not create a BUY signal. A bearish EMA trend alone does not create a SELL signal.

The scanner discovers all available Binance spot USDT pairs, then scans the highest-volume pairs first. Binance has API rate limits, so scanning every listed pair with candles simultaneously is not reliable. Change MAX_SCAN_COINS to adjust the number scanned per cycle.

Signals are estimates, not guaranteed turning points or trading advice. Historical backtests are simplified and do not represent real fills. This page does not execute trades. Spot SELL signals are not executed short trades.

Public market data: Binance. Charts: TradingView. News: external RSS service. API availability depends on your network and region.
