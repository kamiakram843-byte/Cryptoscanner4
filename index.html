📖 Engine methodology, calculations & risk disclosure

BTC projection: Uses BTC 15m taker flow, 1H ATR, 1H/4H-style swing levels, current order-book liquidity concentrations, funding, OI and catalyst sentiment. The projection is a heuristic bounded by nearby liquidity and volatility estimates.

True beta: Covariance of coin and BTC 15m log returns divided by BTC return variance over 32 observations. A minimum variance check prevents unstable ratios.

Whale volume: Current completed candle volume divided by the average of preceding completed candles. This is an unusual-volume proxy, not proof of whale identity.

Entry: BTC projected percentage move multiplied by beta, blended with the coin's own ATR and swing level. Entries are hypothetical future limit prices, not market orders.

Stop loss: 1.2 × ATR from the proposed entry. Target 1 uses a 1.5 × ATR move. Target 2 uses a swing/liquidity reference where available.

Confidence: A weighted heuristic based on beta, flow, volume, BTC alignment, funding and news. It is not a statistically calibrated probability of success.

Limitations: Public REST snapshots do not provide full historical order-book depth, order cancellations, exchange-wide liquidations or verified institutional identities. The app does not submit orders.
