10 / ACTIVE STRATEGY RULES

BTC BOTTOM BOUNCE: closed BTC 15M candle pierces below its lower Bollinger Band, closes back inside, lower wick at least 50% of body, and intersects a detected horizontal support. This opens LONG clearance.

BTC PEAK REJECTION: closed BTC 15M candle pierces above its upper Bollinger Band, closes back inside, upper wick at least 50% of body, and intersects a detected horizontal resistance. This opens SHORT clearance.

BTC SIDEWAYS: BTC is between its 15M bands with relatively flat 15M and 1H changes. A green candle rising above the middle EMA20 opens LONG-only clearance; a red candle falling below the middle EMA20 opens SHORT-only clearance.

ALTCOIN CONFIRMATION: bottom gate requires a 15M lower-band rejection wick; peak gate requires an upper-band rejection wick. Sideways clearance requires a matching candle direction and a rejection of local 15M support or resistance.

LEVELS: EP is the closed 15M confirmation candle close. SL is 0.5% beyond its wick tip. TP1 is 15M EMA20; TP2 is 1H EMA20; TP3 is the nearest 4H structural pivot. Signals with invalid or incorrectly ordered targets are rejected.

TRACKER: every unique signal has a 120-minute lifespan. TP1 moves the tracker stop to entry. SL, TP2/TP3, and time limit close the tracker state.
