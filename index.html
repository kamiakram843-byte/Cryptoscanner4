How to interpret the targets

Entry zone: potential price range; wait for confirmation.
SL: estimated invalidation / stop-loss level.
TP1, TP2, TP3: progressively more distant price targets.
Move %: estimated distance from the entry-zone midpoint.
Targets are volatility-based estimates, not predictions or guarantees.
