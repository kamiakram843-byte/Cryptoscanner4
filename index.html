How to Read Signals

BUY: potential spot entry based on bullish technical conditions. SELL: potential spot exit / bearish conditions. LONG: potential futures long setup. SHORT: potential futures short setup. BULLISH: broader upward trend. BEARISH: broader downward trend.

Score is a rule-based technical estimate, not a probability of profit. Entry, stop-loss and targets are estimates derived from recent candle data. Crypto can move rapidly, and stop-loss orders are not guaranteed to fill at the exact price. This scanner does not place trades.
