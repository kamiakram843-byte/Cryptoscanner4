Scanner methodology

The scanner evaluates market structure, relative volume, taker buy/sell flow, order-book imbalance, spread, VWAP, RSI, momentum, ATR and available Futures open-interest/funding information. Scores rank the strength of the evidence; they are not win probabilities. Order-book snapshots can change quickly and visible walls can be cancelled. Always verify liquidity and risk before trading. This tool does not place orders.
