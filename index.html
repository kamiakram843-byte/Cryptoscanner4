Strategy rules

A — Panic Dip (LONG)
Previous closed 15m candle closes below lower Bollinger Band with RSI below 30. Latest closed candle returns inside the bands and has a lower rejection wick.
B — Routine Low (LONG)
Latest closed 15m candle sweeps a confirmed 1h/4h pivot support and closes back above it, lower wick at least 50% of body, RSI 30–42.
C — Panic Peak (SHORT)
Previous closed 15m candle closes above upper Bollinger Band with RSI above 75. Latest candle returns inside and confirms bearish RSI divergence.
D — Routine Peak (SHORT)
Latest closed 15m candle sweeps a confirmed 1h/4h pivot resistance and closes below it, upper wick at least 50% of body, volume below preceding green candles.
