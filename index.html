Scanner Rules

LONG triggerCompleted 1H close above SMA20, SMA20 above SMA50, and RSI crosses upward through 30.
SHORT triggerCompleted 1H close below SMA20, SMA20 below SMA50, and RSI crosses downward through 70.
BTC master filterBTC price above daily SMA200 prioritizes LONG; below SMA200 prioritizes SHORT. The opposite direction is not automatically blocked.
Risk calculatorStop distance is 2% of entry. TP1 = 1R, TP2 = 2R, TP3 = 3.5R. These are mathematical levels, not forecasts.
Funding confirmationLONGs are blocked when available funding is above +0.05% (0.0005). Missing funding data is marked unavailable, not confirmed.
Timeframe / refreshUses closed 1H candles. Full scan refresh interval is 15 minutes. Signals are not automatically traded.

API / Safety Information

This page uses public Binance market-data endpoints. No API key is required and no orders are placed. Futures funding and open interest are optional context and may be unavailable for some spot pairs. All signals are educational technical indicators, not financial advice.

Binance limits requests. The scanner uses controlled concurrency and backs off after rate-limit errors. Keep this page open for scanning; signals and history are held in browser memory and are not saved permanently.
