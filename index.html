How to read the signals

LONG reversal: Price approaches support, then shows signs of buying pressure and a possible bounce. EP is near support until confirmation appears.

LONG breakout: Price moves above resistance with volume confirmation. EP is the breakout trigger or current price after confirmation.

SHORT rejection: Price approaches resistance and shows signs of selling pressure. EP is near resistance until confirmation appears.

SHORT breakdown: Price moves below support with volume confirmation. EP is the breakdown trigger or current price after confirmation.

All signals are technical estimates, not verified whale trades or guaranteed predictions. Always check the chart and risk before trading.
