Signal Rules

BUY: BTC bullish + coin bullish trend + pullback + rebound confirmation + peak filter.
SELL: BTC bearish + coin bearish trend + pullback + breakdown confirmation.
LONG: Futures bullish setup.
SHORT: Futures bearish setup.
Peak filter: Coin must have sufficient distance from its 30-day and 90-day highs.
No signal when BTC is sideways or market data is incomplete.

All signals are analytical estimates, not guaranteed trades.
