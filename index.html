Levels are calculated from completed candles. Pullback entry uses EMA20 in a qualifying trend; rebound entry uses a support/band recovery setup. SL is based on the setup's invalidation level and ATR buffer. Targets use risk multiples of 1R, 2R and 3R. Planned entries are not guaranteed fills. Short setups are analytical only and do not mean Binance Spot supports opening a short position.

How to read the levels

LONG: bullish trend with a qualifying pullback or rebound. SHORT: bearish analytical setup. NEUTRAL: no qualifying directional setup.

A price level is a calculation, not a prediction. Confirm support/resistance, liquidity, spread, volume, and candle confirmation before considering a trade. Fees, slippage, gaps, and volatility can change actual risk/reward.
