🧠 STRATEGY RULES
EARLY REVERSAL / RELATIVE STRENGTH LONG

• Rejection from a recent support area
• Higher low or bullish RSI divergence
• MACD momentum improving
• Bollinger middle-band recovery
• Coin outperforming BTC
• Volume expansion and bullish candle
• No requirement for RSI to be above 50
EARLY REVERSAL / RELATIVE STRENGTH SHORT

• Rejection from a recent resistance area
• Lower high or bearish RSI divergence
• MACD momentum weakening
• Bollinger middle-band failure
• Coin underperforming BTC
• Volume expansion and bearish candle
• No requirement for RSI to be below 50
Scores are technical screening scores, not win probabilities. Early reversal signals are less established than trend signals. Wait for confirmation and manage risk independently.
