Signal methodology and limitations
Signals use EMA trend, RSI, ATR and recent candle structure. “Confirmed” means the programmed rules were met, not that a trade will win. TP and SL estimates must be checked against spread, fees, liquidity and actual market structure. Spot SELL means a sell/exit watchlist; it does not open a short position. Futures LONG and SHORT are analysis labels only.
