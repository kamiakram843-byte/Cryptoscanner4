How the scanner decides

BTC filter: BTC 1H EMA20/50 and 15M EMA9/21 plus RSI are checked. If BTC direction is mixed or neutral, new trades are blocked.

Coin filter: Each coin is checked using 1H trend, 15M trend, RSI, 5M price/EMA and relative strength versus BTC.

LONG: BTC bullish + coin bullish + valid support rejection or breakout + volume/taker-buy confirmation.

SHORT: BTC bearish + coin bearish + valid resistance rejection or breakdown + volume/taker-sell confirmation.

Early watch: The price is approaching support/resistance or a breakout/breakdown level. It is not a confirmed entry.

Important: This scanner uses candle data and taker-buy volume as a flow proxy. It does not claim to measure all exchange inflows, whale activity, or order-book liquidity.
